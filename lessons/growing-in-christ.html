BIBLE STUDIES • LESSON 10

Growing in Christ

Learn how Christian growth develops through God's Word, prayer, faith, obedience, character, community and a life increasingly centered on Christ.

1. What Does It Mean to Grow in Christ?

Growing in Christ means continually developing in our relationship with God and allowing our faith to influence our character, decisions and everyday life.

Christian growth is not simply about knowing more Bible verses. It involves becoming more mature in the way we think, speak, respond and live.

Key idea:

Christian growth is a journey of becoming more mature in faith and increasingly reflecting the character of Christ.

2. Spiritual Growth Takes Time

Growth normally happens progressively. Just as a physical person develops over time, spiritual maturity also involves learning, practice, correction and perseverance.

2 Peter 3:18

Peter encourages believers to grow in the grace and knowledge of Jesus Christ.

Do not measure your entire journey by one difficult day. Look at the direction of your life and the lessons you are learning along the way.

3. God's Word

Scripture provides instruction, wisdom, encouragement and correction.

2 Timothy 3:16–17

Paul describes Scripture as useful for teaching, correction, training and equipping people for good works.

Regular Bible study can help develop a stronger foundation for Christian living.

4. Prayer and Relationship With God

Prayer is more than asking God for things. It is also a way of expressing gratitude, confession, worship, dependence and seeking guidance.

1 Thessalonians 5:17

Paul encourages believers to pray continually.

Build consistency, not performance.

A growing prayer life develops through regular communication with God rather than trying to impress other people.

5. Faith and Obedience

Faith is not merely agreeing that certain statements are true. Christian faith is intended to influence how a person lives.

James 2:17

James teaches that faith without corresponding action is dead.

Growth therefore involves learning what God teaches and putting that teaching into practice.

6. Areas of Christian Growth

📖

Bible

Develop a deeper understanding of Scripture and learn to apply it responsibly.

🙏

Prayer

Develop a consistent relationship with God through prayer and dependence on Him.

❤️

Character

Develop qualities such as patience, humility, honesty, love and self-control.

🤝

Relationships

Learn to treat other people with forgiveness, respect, compassion and wisdom.

🛡️

Self-Control

Learn to manage desires, emotions and impulses rather than allowing them to control your actions.

🛠️

Service

Use your abilities, time and resources to contribute to the wellbeing of others.

7. The Fruit of the Spirit

Galatians 5:22–23 describes qualities associated with the fruit of the Spirit.

Galatians 5:22–23

Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control.

These qualities provide a useful framework for examining character growth.

Ask yourself not only, "How much do I know?" but also, "What kind of person am I becoming?"

8. Growth Through Difficulties

Difficult experiences can expose weaknesses, develop perseverance and teach us to depend on God.

James 1:2–4

James explains that the testing of faith can produce perseverance and maturity.

This does not mean every difficult experience is good in itself. It means difficulties can become opportunities for learning, endurance and growth.

9. Learning From Mistakes

Growth does not require pretending that we never make mistakes.

A mature response to failure is to recognize the mistake, take responsibility, seek forgiveness where necessary and learn how to make better choices.

A Growth Mindset

Instead of saying:

"I failed, so I can never change."

Ask:

"What happened, what can I learn, and what can I change next time?"

10. Christian Community

Christian growth is not intended to happen entirely in isolation.

Healthy relationships with other believers can provide encouragement, correction, wisdom, accountability and opportunities to serve.

Hebrews 10:24–25

The passage encourages believers to consider how they can encourage one another toward love and good works and not neglect meeting together.

11. Guard What Influences You

Growth can be affected by the content we consume, the people we spend time with and the habits we practice.

  • Choose content that strengthens your mind.
  • Be thoughtful about the people influencing your decisions.
  • Avoid repeatedly feeding destructive habits.
  • Make time for learning and reflection.
  • Create routines that support your goals.

12. Developing Christian Discipline

Discipline means repeatedly choosing what is valuable even when you do not feel motivated.

01

Study

Set aside regular time to read and understand Scripture.

02

Pray

Maintain regular times of prayer and reflection.

03

Practice

Apply what you learn through everyday decisions.

04

Reflect

Review your choices and identify areas where you need to grow.

13. Love as a Sign of Growth

Christian maturity is strongly connected with love for God and love for other people.

John 13:34–35

Jesus taught His disciples to love one another and described this love as a distinguishing mark of His followers.

Growth should therefore affect how we treat people, not merely what we know.

14. Becoming More Like Christ

Christian growth ultimately points toward becoming more like Christ in character and conduct.

Romans 12:2

Paul speaks about transformation through the renewing of the mind.

Transformation begins with how we think and eventually affects how we live.

15. A Simple Daily Growth Routine

The 4-Part Daily Routine

  • Read: Spend time in Scripture.
  • Pray: Talk honestly with God.
  • Apply: Put one lesson into practice.
  • Reflect: Examine your actions and identify one area for improvement.

16. A 30-Day Growth Challenge

For the next 30 days, build a simple consistent routine around your relationship with God.

EVERY DAY

Bible

Read and reflect on a portion of Scripture.

EVERY DAY

Prayer

Spend intentional time talking with God.

EVERY DAY

Character

Choose one character quality to practice.

EVERY WEEK

Service

Find at least one practical opportunity to help someone.

17. Questions for Personal Growth

  • Am I becoming more consistent in studying Scripture?
  • Is my prayer life becoming more intentional?
  • What character quality needs the most attention?
  • How do I respond when I am corrected?
  • Am I becoming more patient and self-controlled?
  • How do I treat people when nobody is watching?
  • What influences should I reduce?
  • What healthy habit should I develop?
  • How can I serve other people better?
  • What is God teaching me during this season of my life?

18. Key Scriptures

  • 2 Peter 3:18 — Grow in grace and knowledge
  • 2 Timothy 3:16–17 — Scripture equips believers
  • Galatians 5:22–23 — Fruit of the Spirit
  • James 1:2–4 — Perseverance and maturity
  • Romans 12:2 — Renewal of the mind
  • John 13:34–35 — Love one another
  • Hebrews 10:24–25 — Encourage one another
Final thought:

Do not compare your journey with someone else's. Focus on faithfully learning, practicing, correcting, serving and growing one day at a time.
