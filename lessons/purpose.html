BIBLE STUDY • LESSON 06

Purpose & Calling

Discover how Scripture speaks about purpose, service, gifts, responsibility and living a life that honors God.

Introduction

Many people ask, "What is my purpose?" The Bible encourages us to look beyond personal success and consider how our lives can be used to love God, serve others and do what is good.

Purpose is not necessarily about having fame, wealth or a particular career. A meaningful Christian life can be expressed through faithfulness in everyday responsibilities and through service to other people.

"For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do."

Ephesians 2:10

1. Our First Purpose Is to Know and Love God

Christian purpose begins with our relationship with God. Jesus taught that loving God is central to faithful living.

"Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind."

Matthew 22:37

2. We Are Called to Love Others

Our purpose is not only about ourselves. Jesus connected love for God with love for our neighbors.

"And the second is like it: 'Love your neighbor as yourself.'"

Matthew 22:39

3. Everyone Has Different Gifts

People have different abilities, opportunities and responsibilities. Scripture teaches that different gifts can be used to serve others.

You do not need to copy another person's calling. Instead, discover how your own abilities and circumstances can be used for good.

"We have different gifts, according to the grace given to each of us."

Romans 12:6

4. Purpose Includes Service

Jesus demonstrated servant leadership. Christian purpose should therefore include a willingness to help other people rather than focusing only on personal achievement.

"Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant."

Matthew 20:26

5. Be Faithful in What You Have

Sometimes people wait for a major opportunity before they begin serving. Scripture encourages faithfulness in the responsibilities already placed before us.

Small acts of honesty, kindness, responsibility and service can have great significance.

"Whoever can be trusted with very little can also be trusted with much."

Luke 16:10

6. Purpose Requires Action

Understanding your purpose is only the beginning. Purpose becomes meaningful when it influences your decisions and actions.

  • Learn and develop your abilities.
  • Serve people around you.
  • Take responsibility for what God has placed in your hands.
  • Use your skills in constructive ways.
  • Remain willing to learn and change.
  • Seek wisdom before making major decisions.

Finding Your Direction

Finding direction can take time. Pay attention to your abilities, interests, opportunities, responsibilities and the needs around you. Then consider how these things can be used to serve God and people.

Do not assume that purpose must be discovered all at once. A person's direction can develop through different seasons of life.

"Commit to the LORD whatever you do, and he will establish your plans."

Proverbs 16:3

Reflection Questions

  • What abilities or skills has God given you?
  • How can you use your abilities to help others?
  • What responsibilities are already in your hands?
  • Where could you begin serving today rather than waiting for a perfect opportunity?
  • What kind of person do you want to become as you pursue your purpose?
