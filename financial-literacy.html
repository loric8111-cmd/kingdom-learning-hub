Why Financial Literacy Matters

Understanding money is an important life skill.

Financial knowledge helps you make better decisions.

Financial literacy is the ability to understand and manage money responsibly.

It involves learning how to earn, spend, save, borrow, invest and plan for the future.

The goal is not simply to have more money. The goal is to develop the knowledge and discipline needed to use money wisely.
