BIBLE STUDY • LESSON 05

Christian Character

Discover how Christian character is shaped by love, humility, forgiveness, self-control and a life that reflects the teachings of Christ.

Introduction

Christian character describes the qualities and attitudes that should develop in the life of a person who follows Christ.

Following Jesus is not only about what we say we believe. Our faith should also influence the way we treat people, respond to difficulties, make decisions and live each day.

"Therefore, as God's chosen people, holy and dearly loved, clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience."

Colossians 3:12

1. Love

Love is one of the most important characteristics of Christian life. Jesus taught His followers to love God and to love other people.

"Love your neighbor as yourself."

Matthew 22:39

2. Humility

Humility means recognizing that we do not know everything and that we should not consider ourselves more important than others.

A humble person is willing to learn, listen, serve and acknowledge their mistakes.

"Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit. Rather, in humility value others above yourselves."

Philippians 2:3

3. Forgiveness

Christian character includes learning to forgive. Forgiveness does not mean pretending that wrong actions never happened. It means refusing to allow bitterness and revenge to control our hearts.

"Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as in Christ God forgave you."

Ephesians 4:32

4. Self-Control

Self-control involves learning to manage our thoughts, words, emotions and actions. It helps us avoid decisions that can damage ourselves or other people.

"For the Spirit God gave us does not make us timid, but gives us power, love and self-discipline."

2 Timothy 1:7

5. Integrity

Integrity means seeking to live honestly and consistently, even when nobody is watching. Christian character should influence both public behavior and private decisions.

"Whoever walks in integrity walks securely, but whoever takes crooked paths will be found out."

Proverbs 10:9

6. Serving Others

Jesus taught His followers that greatness is connected with serving others. Christian character should therefore produce a willingness to help, encourage and serve people.

"Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant."

Matthew 20:26

How Can Christian Character Grow?

Character develops over time. It requires intentional choices, reflection, prayer, learning from Scripture and practicing what we have learned.

  • Study the character of Jesus in the Gospels.
  • Practice honesty in everyday situations.
  • Learn to control your words and reactions.
  • Forgive instead of holding onto bitterness.
  • Treat other people with dignity and respect.
  • Be willing to admit mistakes and learn from them.
  • Look for opportunities to serve others.

Reflection Questions

  • Which Christian character trait do you most want to develop?
  • How do you normally respond when someone hurts or disappoints you?
  • Where do you need more self-control?
  • How can you demonstrate love to someone this week?
  • What is one practical change you can make to become more Christ-like?
