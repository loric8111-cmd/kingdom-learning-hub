BIBLE STUDY • LESSON 02

Knowing God

Discover what Scripture teaches about God's character, His relationship with humanity, and how we can grow in knowing Him.

Introduction

Knowing God is at the heart of the Christian journey. Christianity is not only about knowing information about God; it is about developing a relationship with Him through faith, obedience, prayer and His Word.

The Bible gives us a foundation for understanding God's character and His relationship with people.

"And this is eternal life, that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent."

John 17:3

1. God Is Creator

Scripture presents God as the Creator of heaven and earth. Everything we see exists because of His creative work.

"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

Genesis 1:1

2. God Is Holy

The Bible teaches that God is holy. His holiness shows His complete purity and separation from sin. This should encourage believers to approach Him with reverence and humility.

"Holy, holy, holy is the LORD Almighty; the whole earth is full of his glory."

Isaiah 6:3

3. God Is Love

One of the central teachings of Scripture is that God's character is defined by love. His love is demonstrated through His actions toward humanity.

"Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love."

1 John 4:8

4. God Desires a Relationship With Us

God does not simply want people to know facts about Him. Scripture repeatedly calls people to seek Him, trust Him and walk with Him.

"Come near to God and he will come near to you."

James 4:8

5. How Can We Know God Better?

Growing in knowledge of God is a continuous journey. Christians can pursue this through Scripture, prayer, faith, obedience and reflection on God's work.

  • Study the Bible regularly.
  • Spend time in prayer.
  • Seek to obey what Scripture teaches.
  • Reflect on God's character.
  • Practice love, humility and forgiveness.
  • Continue learning and asking questions.

Reflection Questions

  • What do you currently understand about God's character?
  • Which biblical description of God speaks most strongly to you?
  • How can you develop a deeper relationship with God?
  • What changes could you make in your daily life to seek God more intentionally?
